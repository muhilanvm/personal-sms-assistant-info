# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Personal SMS Assistant, a personal development project owned by Muhilan Vamadevan.

The assistant uses the mobile number voluntarily enrolled by the project owner to receive SMS requests and send replies, task acknowledgments, progress updates, clarification questions, and completion or error notices.

Message frequency varies with requests. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop messages or HELP for assistance.

Mobile number and SMS consent information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to operate the assistant and deliver requested replies.

The project is intended for the owner's personal use. No other recipient is authorized. The owner may request removal of the enrolled number by contacting the project owner.
